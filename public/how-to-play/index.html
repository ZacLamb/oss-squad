How to play

Oss! Squad takes a minute to learn. Here’s everything you need for your first run.

1

🧭 Steer your line

Your crew walks onto the mat in a line. Swipe on your phone, or use the arrow keys / WASD on a computer, to turn. You only steer: your grapplers fight on their own.

💡 Don’t bump into the walls or your own tail. It hurts the whole crew, so turn early!

2

🚩 Recruit new grapplers

Each gym hides a recruit flag. Steer into it to add a new grappler to your line, or to give one you already have another star.

💡 A bigger crew hits harder, but a longer line is harder to steer.

3

⬆️ Pick an upgrade after each round

Clear a round and choose one of three upgrades: power up one grappler, or help the whole crew.

💡 Spread upgrades around. Every grappler can reach level 15.

4

🛣️ Choose your path

Between rounds you pick what comes next: a normal round, a rest stop to heal, or a risky round with more rivals and better rewards.

💡 Hurt? Take the rest. Feeling strong? Take the risk.

5

⚡ Fire your special

Beating rivals fills the special meter in the corner. When it’s full, tap it (or press Q) to hit every rival near your crew.

💡 Some crews unlock a secret special. Try a crew of only Wrestlers!

6

🏆 Beat the champions

Every fifth round a champion is waiting, under the Master Spell. Watch their big moves and keep your line moving.

💡 Each champion you free plays the next chapter of the story.

7

🎒 Gear up between rounds

Open Crew between rounds to spend points, swap gear and check everyone’s stats. Crew and gear changes happen only between rounds.

💡 Better gear goes to the grappler who needs it most.

8

🎖️ Climb the belts

Coins you earn are kept even if your crew taps out. Spend them at Camp. Beat the whole circuit to start earning belts in the Legacy Hall, from White all the way to Red.

💡 Each new belt brings real rewards, and some belts change how your crew looks.

❓ FAQ

Is Oss! Squad free?

Yes. It is completely free to play, with no ads, no in-game purchases and no sign-up.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It plays in your web browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. It is a cartoon grappling adventure with no chat, no ads and no blood. Kids Mode removes wall bumps, softens hits and adds checkpoints, and Tag Team lets two players share one device.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Progress saves automatically on your device, and Cloud Save in Settings gives you a code to carry it to another device.

How long does a run take?

Each round takes under a minute. The full circuit is 50 rounds with 10 champions, and you can save and quit between rounds.

Which languages are supported?

English and Portuguese (Brazil), including the narrated story.
