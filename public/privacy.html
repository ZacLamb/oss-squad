Privacy

Oss! Squad is a free browser game. We do not ask for your name, email or any account.

What is stored

Your progress (unlocked grapplers, coins, belts, settings) is saved on your own device using your browser’s local storage. If you use Cloud Save, a copy of that progress is stored under a random code so you can load it on another device. It contains no personal information.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads, no in-game purchases and no third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

Kids

The game has no chat and no way to contact other players, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the team that runs this site.
