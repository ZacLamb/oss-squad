How to play

Everlight Heroes takes a minute to learn. Here’s everything you need for your first run.

Jump to: Steer your line Grab banners to recruit heroes Pick an upgrade after each floor Choose your path Fire your Nova Beat the bosses Manage your party Spend coins at Camp FAQ

1 Steer your line

Your heroes march in a line, like a conga line. Swipe on your phone, or use the arrow keys / WASD on a computer, to turn. You only steer: the heroes attack monsters on their own.

💡 Don’t bump into walls or your own tail. It hurts the whole party, so turn early!

2 Grab banners to recruit heroes

Each floor hides a golden recruit banner. Steer into it to add a new hero to your line, or to power up one you already have with stars.

💡 A full party is stronger, but a longer line is harder to steer. Choose what fits you.

3 Pick an upgrade after each floor

Clear a floor and choose one of three upgrades: power up a single hero, or help the whole party. Heroes level up as you pick upgrades for them.

💡 Each hero can reach level 15, so spread upgrades around your party.

4 Choose your path

Between floors you pick the road ahead: a steady path, a camp to heal up, or a risky shrine with more monsters and bigger rewards.

💡 Hurt? Take the camp. Feeling strong? Try the shrine.

5 Fire your Nova

Defeating monsters fills the NOVA meter in the corner. When it’s full, tap it (or press Q) to blast every monster near your heroes.

💡 Certain parties change the Nova into a secret super move. Try a team of only Knights!

6 Beat the bosses

Every fifth floor has a boss guarding a shard of the Everlight. Watch for their special attacks and keep your line moving.

💡 Each boss you beat unlocks the next chapter of the story.

7 Manage your party

Between floors, open Party to spend level points, swap gear, and see every hero’s stats.

💡 Better gear equips itself on the hero who needs it most.

8 Spend coins at Camp

Coins you earn are saved even if your party falls. Spend them at Camp to unlock new heroes and permanent upgrades.

💡 Every run makes the next one stronger, so keep going!

Playing with kids?

Turn on Kids Mode from the main menu: no wall bumps, gentle auto-steering, softer hits and checkpoints. Tag Team lets two players take turns on one device, switching every floor.

Frequently asked questions

Is Everlight Heroes free?

Yes. It is completely free to play, with no ads, no in-game purchases and no sign-up.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It plays right in your web browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. It is a cartoon fantasy adventure with no chat and no ads. Kids Mode removes wall bumps, softens hits, gently auto-steers and adds checkpoints, and Tag Team lets two players share one device.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Progress saves automatically on your device. Cloud Save in Settings gives you a code to carry your heroes to another device.

How long does a run take?

Each floor takes under a minute. A full climb is 50 floors with 10 bosses, and you can save and quit between floors at any time.

Which languages are supported?

English and Portuguese (Brazil), including the narrated story.
