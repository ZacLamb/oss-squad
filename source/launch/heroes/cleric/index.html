The Cleric

A kind healer who carries a lantern of holy light. The Cleric keeps everyone standing, healing the most hurt hero and calling down smites that heal too.

Unlock at Camp for 400 coins, or recruit one from a banner during a run.

Toughness
Damage
Range
Attack speed

How they fight

Holy smites that strike a monster and heal your weakest hero.

Special power: Blessing

Heals the most hurt hero, and every smite heals as well.

Skill: Sanctuary

A healing aura follows the Cleric for 5 seconds. Unlocks as the Cleric levels up.

Upgrades

  • Greater Heal+50% healing power.
  • Mass HealEvery heal also mends the whole party a little.

Best team-ups

Put these heroes in the same party for a bonus to everyone:

Knight · Holy Bulwark−6% damage taken
Mage · Mana Well+8% damage, +15% healing
Bard · Choir+20% healing, +4% attack speed

Secret Nova: Holy Light

Build a party of only Clerics and your Nova blast turns into Holy Light: full heal for everyone, and the light burns nearby monsters.

Tip

One Cleric makes the deep floors much easier. Recruit one early if you find a Cleric banner.
