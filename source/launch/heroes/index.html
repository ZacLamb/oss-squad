Seven heroes, seven ways to fight. Mix them in your party, power them up, and find the team-ups that work for you. Tap a hero to see their powers, upgrades, best team-ups and secret Nova move.

How heroes join your party

You start with the Knight, Archer and Mage. Every floor hides a recruit banner: grab it to add a hero to your line or power up one you already have. Unlock the Cleric, Rogue, Bard and Necromancer at Camp with the coins you earn.
