Hero guide · Support

The Bard

A happy musician who never stops playing. The Bard’s songs make every hero faster and stronger, and gently heal the whole party.

Unlock at Camp for 700 coins, or recruit one from a banner during a run.

Toughness
Damage
Range
Attack speed

How they fight

Musical notes that bounce at monsters.

Special power: Battle Song

The party gets +8% attack speed and +10% damage. Every 5 seconds a song heals everyone a little.

Skill: Crescendo

The whole party attacks 35% faster for 5 seconds. Unlocks as the Bard levels up.

Upgrades

  • AnthemThe whole party attacks 8% faster.
  • Encore+1 note per song.

Best team-ups

Put these heroes in the same party for a bonus to everyone:

Cleric · Choir+20% healing, +4% attack speed
Rogue · Wandering Band+6% crit, +5% attack speed

Secret Nova: Encore

Build a party of only Bards and your Nova blast turns into Encore: a sound wave dazes monsters and gives +35% attack speed.

Tip

The Bard makes every other hero better. The bigger your party, the more the Bard helps.
