The Rogue

A quick, sneaky fighter with twin daggers. The Rogue attacks faster than anyone and lands huge critical hits.

Unlock at Camp for 500 coins, or recruit one from a banner during a run.

Toughness
Damage
Range
Attack speed

How they fight

Lightning-fast dagger strikes up close.

Special power: Backstab

Critical hits deal 2.5× damage.

Skill: Blade Dance

Spins through nearby monsters, shredding up to six. Unlocks as the Rogue levels up.

Upgrades

  • Assassin+15% critical chance.
  • FlurryEach strike hits +1 more monster.

Best team-ups

Put these heroes in the same party for a bonus to everyone:

Archer · Deadly Duo+8% critical chance
Knight · Flank+6% attack speed
Bard · Wandering Band+6% crit, +5% attack speed

Secret Nova: Shadow Strike

Build a party of only Rogues and your Nova blast turns into Shadow Strike: shadow strikes hit the toughest monsters.

Tip

The Rogue is great against bosses: give them crit upgrades and watch the big numbers fly.
